## Claim Guardrail

The paper may use literature to explain why hierarchy and context control matter. It must not claim speed or quality superiority over external systems unless Person 1 produces same-scene, same-query evidence. Paper-reported runtimes from different datasets should stay in related work, not in the results table.

## Article Framing

Beyond Proximity should be framed as a rendering-first semantic hierarchy over captured views and semantic items. The defensible comparison is internal same-input graph traversal versus internal same-input flat retrieval.

| Article need | Closest works | Safe wording |
|---|---|---|
| Closest current direction | HOV-SG, BBQ, OpenLex3D | "These works motivate hierarchical and benchmark-aware open-vocabulary 3D reasoning." |
| Language-field / 3DGS semantic representation context | LERF, LangSplat, Semantic Gaussians, LEGS | "These methods ground language in radiance fields or Gaussians; our current evidence is graph traversal over semantic items." |
| Future 3D localization and segmentation metrics | ScanRefer, OpenMask3D, OpenLex3D | "3D IoU, AP, AP25, AP50, mAP, and mIoU require GT boxes, masks, semantic labels, or external datasets." |
| Token/context metric support | AttentionRAG, Provence, TeaRAG, S-Path-RAG, prompt-compression survey | "Token and context metrics are supported by RAG and prompt-compression literature, not by standard 3D scene benchmarks." |
| Current measured results | `flat_lexical`, `graph_traversal`, optional `flat_embedding`, optional `graph_affordance_pruned` | "We compare methods that run over the same scenes, semantic items, query strings, and verified labels." |

External papers must not be presented as measured baselines unless same-scene, same-query adapters and saved result JSON exist.

The reviewed 3D scene understanding papers do not use LLM token count as a primary evaluation metric. Token/context papers support our internal context-size and token-efficiency metrics, while 3D papers support semantic scene understanding, graph hierarchy, language fields, and future 3D metrics.

## Paper-Ready Limitations

| Limitation | Why it belongs in the article |
|---|---|
| External methods are not run in this sprint | Avoids unfair comparisons across datasets and setups. |
| Current captured scenes do not have object-level 3D boxes for ScanRefer/OpenLex3D-style metrics | Prevents unsupported 3D IoU or localization claims. |
| mIoU/AP metrics require masks or semantic labels | Prevents unsupported segmentation accuracy claims. |
| Token count is not a standard 3D scene understanding metric | Token/context metrics must be framed as internal graph-vs-flat efficiency metrics. |
| Language-field methods optimize a different representation | Their native outputs are relevancy maps, not tree traversal traces. |
| Graph methods depend on segmentation and association | Their failure modes differ from VLM-summary/tree failure modes. |
