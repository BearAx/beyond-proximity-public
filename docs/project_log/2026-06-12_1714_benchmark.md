## Failure / room-disambiguation cases

| | Graph room-correct | Flat room-correct | Flat wrong room | Graph wins |
|--|-------------------:|------------------:|----------------:|-----------:|
| Count | 4/5 | 2/5 | 2 | 2 |

**Key demo query:** *"where is the screen in the conference room"* — graph finds the screen in the correct room; flat often returns a screen from a different zone.

### Case-by-case

| Query | Graph view | Graph verdict | Flat view | Flat verdict | Graph wins? |
|-------|------------|---------------|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| where is the screen in the conference room | v017 | correct_room | v012 | wrong_room | ✓ |
| where is the screen in the conference room located | v017 | correct_room | v012 | wrong_room | ✓ |
| where is the projection screen in the ballroom | v017 | correct_room | v012 | correct_room | — |
| find the projector in the ballroom | v013 | correct_room | v013 | correct_room | — |
| where is the exit sign in the lobby | — | miss | — | miss | — |

### Reasoning — why graph helps

**where is the screen in the conference room**

- Room target: Conference room / stage (presentation end)
- Expected views: `v017, v018`
- Common flat mistake: `v012, v015`
- Multiple projection screens exist in the hall. Flat search returns the first keyword match (often v012 — ballroom floor through doors) without respecting the room constraint. Graph traversal enters the stage/screen leaf and confirms v017/v018.

**where is the screen in the conference room located**

- Room target: Conference room / stage (presentation end)
- Expected views: `v017, v018`
- Common flat mistake: `v012, v015`
- Same room constraint with natural phrasing ('located'). Tests whether filler words break matching and whether graph still scopes correctly.
